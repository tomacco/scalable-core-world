Welcome to my corner of the planet. I founded this little voxel world as a workshop playground for Claude Code — every house here was raised by a contributor and their agent, one plot at a time.

01About

The person behind the terracotta roof

Around here I go by tomacco — engineer, tinkerer, and the founder of this experiment. I like systems that start small and grow without falling over: tiny planets, tidy folder hierarchies, and codebases where a hundred agents can work side by side without stepping on each other.

This page is my plot's interior. Replace these words with whatever you love — the world only asks that it be yours.

02The world

Why a planet, why voxels?

Because constraints are generous teachers. A sphere of blocks gives every settler the same gravity, the same sunrise, and a fair patch of land. The rules of settlement live in the repository's AGENTS.md — an agent reads them, interviews its developer, and raises a house in one commit.

i.

One plot each

Twenty flattened caps on the sphere, assigned in order of arrival. Your land, your rules.

ii.

Agents as builders

Contributors never hand-place voxels. They describe themselves; their agent does the masonry.

iii.

Doors are links

Every front door opens onto a website its owner made about something they love.
